Industry-Specific Retail Applications

Purpose-built software suites designed around unique vertical workflows

Trength™ Jewellery

Precious Metals • Gems • Hallmark HUID
BULLION & ORNAMENTS

Engineered for retail jewellers, bullion traders, and goldsmiths. Offers end-to-end ornament lifecycle tracking, live commodity rate feeds, and precision scale connectivity.

Core Jewellery Capabilities:
  • Live Bullion Feeds: Automatic 24K, 22K, 18K Gold, Fine Silver, and Platinum MCX rate sync.
  • Hallmark HUID Compliance: BIS-mandated 6-character alphanumeric barcode scanning and verification.
  • Digital Weighing Balance Integration: RS232 / USB COM port link for auto-capturing Gross & Net weight with zero manual entry.
  • Ornament Breakdown: Stone deductions, wastage %, making charges per gram/piece, and purity karat conversion.
  • Artisan (Karigar) Ledger: Metal issue/receipt tracking, melting loss reconciliation, and job-order tracking.

Trength™ Mobile

Smartphones • Multi-IMEI • Service Hub
TELECOM & GADGETS

Engineered for multi-brand smartphone retail stores and gadget service centres with serialized inventory and repair job-sheet tracking.

Core Mobile Capabilities:
  • Multi-IMEI & Serial Tracking: Dual-SIM IMEI capture, warranty expiry logs, and instant serial number search.
  • Variant Management Matrix: Organize handsets by Brand • Model • RAM • Storage • Color • Network band.
  • Trade-In & Buyback Valuation: Used smartphone condition assessment checklist with instant customer exchange voucher generation.
  • Service & Repair Job-Sheets: Technician work assignments, spare-parts billing, and customer SMS repair status updates.
  • Accessories & Recharges: Fast barcode scanning for covers, chargers, tempered glass, and prepaid plans.

Trength™ Apparel

Fashion • Footwear • Multi-Grid Matrix
CLOTHING & TEXTILES

Designed for garment showrooms, boutiques, and multi-store fashion chains requiring high-speed barcode checkout and matrix inventory.

Core Clothing Capabilities:
  • Size × Color × Fit Matrix: Multi-dimensional inventory tracking across XS/S/M/L/XL/XXL and seasonal shades.
  • Automated Barcode Tag Printing: Integrated EAN-13 / Code 128 thermal tag printing with MRP, discount, and wash-care details.
  • Fast Promotional Billing: "Buy 2 Get 1 Free", bundle discounts, seasonal end-of-season sales (EOSS), and store credits.
  • Alteration & Tailoring Management: Customer measurement cards, trial dates, and tailor delivery schedules.
  • Inter-Branch Transfers: Seamless warehouse-to-store distribution with computerized transfer challans.

Trength™ Electronics

Appliances • Serialized Stocks • AMC
HOME APPLIANCES

Built for consumer electronics, TV/audio showrooms, IT hardware stores, and home appliance dealers managing warranty contracts and financing.

Core Electronics Capabilities:
  • Serialized Inventory & Batch Control: Track televisions, refrigerators, laptops, and AC units by unique serial barcode.
  • Extended Warranty & AMC: Sell and manage Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and damage protection plans.
  • Consumer Financing & EMI Integration: Native support for Bajaj Finserv, HDFC, and PineLabs credit card EMI schemes.

Platform Architecture & Enterprise Pillars

Robust foundational engineering shared across the entire Trength Suite
Offline-First Resilience

Billing never stops when internet cables get cut. Counters process invoices and print receipts at sub-second speed with local SQLite/SQL ledgers, syncing transactions to the cloud once connectivity restores.

Multi-Counter & Tablet Pairing

Sales associates carry wireless tablets on the showroom floor to assist shoppers, search catalogs, build carts, and hand off instantly to cashier counters for checkout.

GST & Statutory E-Invoicing

Automated multi-slab GST calculation (CGST, SGST, IGST), e-Way Bill JSON generation, QR-code e-invoices, and one-click monthly GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B audit reconciliation.

Windows Service & Command Centre

A dedicated native Windows daemon manages background WebSockets, port bindings (8082), automated daily bullion rate syncs, and self-healing diagnostics.

Hardware Peripheral Ecosystem

Native driver bridges for thermal POS receipt printers (ESC/POS), 2D barcode scanners, customer pole displays, electronic cash drawers, and precision weighing scales.

Omnichannel Cloud Synchronization

Consolidate multi-store stock positions, track store-level profitability, manage staff roles and biometric permissions, and view live executive reports anywhere.
